Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

PlateRank is operated by an individual and is referred to as "we" in this policy. Personal data is processed under Turkish data protection law (KVKK) and, if you are in Europe, the GDPR. In short: we collect only what the service needs, we do not sell data and we do not run ad tracking.

What we collect and why

  • Plan form: email, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goal, wake and sleep times, meal preference and your typical breakfast. Used to calculate and deliver your plan.
  • Health screening questions: your answers about pregnancy or chronic conditions are used only at that moment to decide whether a plan is appropriate and are not stored. No plan is created and nothing is stored for people under 18.
  • Account: your email address and session data for sign-in.
  • Subscription: subscription status, period end date and email received from Polar. Card and bank details are processed only by Polar.
  • Fullness answers: your answers about how long a food kept you full. Used in aggregate to improve food scores.
  • Device ID: when you vote, a random ID generated in your browser is sent so repeat votes from the same device can be filtered. It is not linked to your name or email.
  • Browser storage: your favorites, menu and settings are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent to our servers.
  • Visit statistics: cookieless, aggregate page view counts via Vercel Web Analytics.

Legal basis

Plan and subscription data are processed to enter into and perform our contract with you; health-related details such as height and weight are processed with the explicit consent you give in the form; security and abuse prevention rely on legitimate interest. You can withdraw consent at any time, in which case we cannot calculate your plan.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. These providers process data on our behalf to run the service:

  • Supabase: database and sign-in
  • Vercel: hosting and visit statistics
  • Polar: payments, invoicing and tax

Their servers may be located outside Türkiye (in the EU or US). Transfers happen only as needed to provide the service and under the providers' data protection commitments. We do not share data with anyone else unless required by law.

How long we keep it

Plan data is kept while your subscription is active and deleted 12 months after it ends, or 12 months after you created the plan if you never subscribed. If you ask us to delete it, we do so within 30 days. Invoice records are kept by Polar for the legally required period.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to object to processing and to receive your data in a portable format. Email your request and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or the data protection authority in your country.

Security

Connections are encrypted. Row-level access rules are enabled in the database, so each user can see only their own plan.

Changes

If we update this policy we will change the date above and email subscribers about material changes.
