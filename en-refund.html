Refunds and cancellation

Last updated: October 21, 2026

In short: if you are not happy, you get your money back.

Refunds

  • First payment: you can request a refund within 14 days of paying. No questions asked.
  • Renewals: if you forgot to cancel, write to us within 7 days of the renewal and we will refund that month.
  • If the service did not work because of a problem on our side, we refund the affected period regardless of timing.

How to request one

Email iletisim@platerank.dev from the address you paid with. Refunds are issued through Polar to your original payment method and may take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

Cancellation

Cancelling stops the next renewal; it is not a refund. You can cancel at any time via the link in your payment email, the Polar customer portal, or by emailing us. You keep access to your plan until the end of the period you paid for.

These terms do not limit any rights you have under the consumer laws of your country.
